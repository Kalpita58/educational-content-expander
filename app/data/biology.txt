Living organisms are made of cells. Cells are the basic units of life. The cell membrane controls what enters and leaves the cell. The nucleus stores genetic information called DNA. Plant cells contain chloroplasts that capture sunlight. Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy. Plants use water and carbon dioxide to make glucose and release oxygen. Animals obtain energy by consuming food. In ecosystems, producers make food, consumers eat other organisms, and decomposers recycle nutrients. A food web shows how energy moves between organisms. Biodiversity helps ecosystems remain resilient when conditions change.
