Learning improves when students connect new ideas to what they already know. Retrieval practice asks learners to recall information from memory. Spaced practice reviews material over several sessions instead of one long study period. A clear question can guide reading and note taking. Students can summarize a passage in their own words and check the summary against the original. Feedback helps learners notice errors and choose a useful next step. A well-supported explanation gives a claim, evidence, and reasoning. Revising a draft can make its ideas clearer to a reader.
